Copy the name, address, and phone exactly as written. Use the same text on every listing.

## Step 4 - Photos (add at least 10)

Use real photos only. Do not show faces or ID numbers.

1. Logo
2. Cover photo: your best plant display
3. Five photos of plants in pots (Money Plant, Snake Plant, ZZ Plant, Peace Lily, Areca Palm)
4. One photo of the nursery entrance
5. One photo of your team at work
6. One photo of a finished office or terrace job (ask the client first)

## Step 5 - Verify by video

Google may ask for a video. Record it on a phone.

- Length: 30 seconds or more. One take. No edits.
- Show the street sign or a landmark near the nursery.
- Show plants and tools.
- Show a branded item, such as the sign, a pot with the logo, or your van.
- Show a service in action, such as watering or potting.
- Do not use a fake address. A failed check can suspend the profile.

After you verify, tell the agent. The agent then marks ticket 84 progress.

## Weekly routine (about 2 hours)

1. Take or pick one real photo (30 minutes).
2. Write the post from the calendar (60 minutes).
3. Post it with a UTM link. Change `week1` to the week number (30 minutes).

## Rules

- Do not give discounts for reviews. Do not pay for reviews.
- Ask a client before you show their office.
- Keep each post under 150 words.
- Check that the name, address, and phone match on every listing.
