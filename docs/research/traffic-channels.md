# Traffic channels for Meri Bagiya

Issue: #72. Map: #70. Date: 2026-10-01.

Scope: Noida first, then Delhi NCR. Lead service for 3 months: office plant rental. Main lead action: WhatsApp chat. Budget: small.

## How to read this

- Cost is the money and time you spend each month.
- Time is the wait before the first lead.
- Lead quality is the chance that a lead becomes a paid client.
- The ranks are judgment calls. No source gives these numbers for a Noida plant nursery. See "What I could not verify".

## Ranking

| Rank | Channel | Cost | Time to first leads | Lead quality | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Business Profile (GBP) | Free | 2 to 6 weeks (after verification) | High | People who search "office plants Noida" want to buy now. |
| 2 | Referrals and direct B2B outreach (WhatsApp, calls, visits) | Free to very low | 1 to 4 weeks | Very high | Office managers trust a known name. A person replies in WhatsApp. |

## Evidence by channel

### 1. Google Business Profile
- Google ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence. Review count and score affect ranking. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
- A business that visits clients (service-area business) must not show a street address if it does not serve clients there. Video verification works for service-area businesses. Rules: 30 seconds or more, unedited, from a mobile device. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705
- Action: set the primary category to plant nursery or similar. Add "Office plant rental" as a service. Add photos, a WhatsApp link, and ask each client for a review.

### 2. Referrals and direct outreach
- No primary source tests this for Indian plant rental. This rank comes from the cost and trust logic of B2B sales, not from a study.
- Competitors list rental terms of up to 3 years for Delhi, Noida and Gurgaon offices (IndiaMART seller page, not neutral): https://m.indiamart.com/growheaven/new-items.html . Buyers expect a contract. A known referral shortens the trust step.

### 3. WhatsApp Business
- The Business app broadcast list caps at 256 contacts. Only people who saved your number get it. Source (secondary): https://telecrm.in/blog/whatsapp-broadcast
- Since 1 July 2025, API messages cost per message. India reports: marketing about Rs 0.78 to Rs 0.86, utility about Rs 0.11 to Rs 0.13, service replies free. Official page: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing . Secondary: https://www.medianama.com/2025/07/223-whatsapp-business-per-message-pricing-india/
- Conclusion: use the free Business app. Do not pay for the API in the first 90 days.

### 4. Google Ads
- The Google Ads WhatsApp extension launched in India in May 2024. Source (secondary): https://ppcnewsfeed.com/ppc-news/2024-08/whatsapp-extension-in-google-ads-rolling-to-more-accounts/
- Use a small cap, a tight Noida location target, and search terms such as "office plants on rent Noida". I found no verified Noida cost per click.

### 5. Instagram and Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with a pre-filled message. Source (secondary): https://d7networks.com/blog/how-to-run-click-to-whatsapp-ads/
- Meta reported that click-to-message ad revenue in India doubled year on year. Source (secondary news): https://sentinelassam.com/business/revenue-from-click-to-message-ads-in-india-has-doubled-meta-founder-672693
- Instagram suits terrace, balcony and gifting offers (visual, home buyers). It suits office rental less.

### 6. Local SEO
- Studies say new pages show first movement in 3 to 6 months. Competitive terms need 12 months or more. Ahrefs reports 1.74% of new pages reach the top 10 in a year. Sources (secondary): https://ahrefs.com/blog/glossary-google-sandbox/ and https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/seo-timeline-new-startup-domain
- Conclusion: keep building the site (service pages for Noida areas). Do not count on it in the first 90 days.

### 7. LinkedIn
- Reported India cost per click: Rs 150 to Rs 650. Minimum budget about Rs 800 per day per campaign. Source (agency blog, not neutral): https://upgrowth.in/linkedin-ads-pricing-by-industry-india/
- That cost is too high for a small budget. Use free organic posts and direct messages to facility and admin managers.

### 8. YouTube
- I found no source that supports it for a local nursery. The rank is a judgment call. Reuse short reels. Add no new work.

### 9. Broadcast and paid API messages
- See channel 3. Use only for past clients and people who ask for updates.

## First 90 days

Days 1 to 7
1. Set up the WhatsApp Business app: profile, catalog (office plant rental packages), quick replies, away message.
2. Create and verify the GBP. Use video verification.
3. Make a list of 50 target offices in Noida. Find the admin or facility manager name.

Days 8 to 30
4. Complete the GBP: services, photos of real office installs, WhatsApp link, one post each week.
5. Send 10 direct WhatsApp or LinkedIn messages each week to the target list. Offer a free site visit.
6. Ask each past client for a Google review. Aim for 10 reviews.
7. Ask each past client for one referral. Offer a small discount for a referral.
8. Check that the website WhatsApp button and lead tracking work (GA4 events).

Days 31 to 60
9. Start Google Ads with a small daily cap. Target Noida only. Use the WhatsApp extension. Run for 4 weeks. Stop if the cost per chat is too high.
10. Post 2 Instagram reels each week from real installs. Reuse them on LinkedIn.
11. Publish 2 website pages: "Office plant rental in Noida" and one for Greater Noida or Ghaziabad.

Days 61 to 90
12. Compare cost per WhatsApp chat, and chats that became a site visit, by channel.
13. Put more time in the best 2 channels. Cut the rest.
14. Test one Meta click-to-WhatsApp ad for terrace or gifting if budget remains.

## What I could not verify

- No source gave cost per click or cost per lead for "office plant rental" in Noida. Test with a small spend.
- No source gave conversion rates for plant rental by channel. Ranks 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 are judgment calls.
- Many cost and timing numbers come from agency blogs. These have a sales interest.
- I did not open the Google Ads help page for the WhatsApp extension. I rely on a news report.
- I did not check the GBP review count of Noida competitors.
- Prices change. Check the Meta and Google pages again before you spend.
