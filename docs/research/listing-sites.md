Issue: MeriBagiya/meri-bagiya-website#73. Map: #70.
Date: 2026-10-01. Goal: leads and calls. Lead service: office plant rental in Noida and Delhi NCR.

## How to read this file

- "Verified" means a first-party page or a search result confirmed the fact.
- "Not verified" means we could not confirm it. Check before you act.
- Prices change. Look at the site on the day you sign up.
- Use one name, address, phone (NAP) on every site. Copy it exactly from Google Business Profile.
- Use a service-area setup (hide the home address) if the nursery is not open to walk-in visitors.

## Rules that apply to all sites

1. Do not buy links. Google forbids buying links for ranking unless the link has rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored". Google also names "low-quality directory or bookmark site links" as link spam. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
2. Do not pay for reviews and do not give discounts for reviews. Google removes such reviews and can restrict the profile. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/14114287
3. Most directory links are nofollow. Their value is leads and trust, not ranking. Not verified for each site.
4. Never sign a yearly auto-debit plan on the first sales call. See the Justdial risk below.

## Site notes and submit steps

### 1. Google Business Profile
- Cost: free.
- Steps: sign in, add the business, pick the category "Plant nursery" or "Landscaper" and add services (Office plant rental, Garden maintenance, Corporate gifting, Terrace garden design). Choose service area (Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Delhi, Gurugram). Verify.
- Verification: record a video on a phone. It must be 30 seconds or longer, unedited and in one take. Show the street sign or landmark, tools or plants, and a branded item or a service in action. Do not show faces or ID numbers. Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/14271705
- Risk: a failed video check suspends the profile. Do not use a fake address.
- Add: photos, WhatsApp link, website link with UTM tag so GA4 shows the source.

### 2. Justdial
- Free listing at https://www.justdial.com/Free-Listing. This page returned HTTP 403 to our fetch tool, so we did not see the form. Not verified by us.
- Paid plans: a secondary summary gave Premium about Rs 15,000-35,000 per year and Assured about Rs 40,000-80,000 per year. Not verified. Ask for the written price. Context: https://startuptalky.com/justdial-grow-your-business/
- Risk: many public complaints say paid plans gave fake enquiries, false top-rank promises, and auto-debit (ECS) problems. See https://www.consumercomplaints.in/justdialcom-b100558 and https://www.complaintsboard.com/just-dial-b126217. These are user claims, not proven facts. Expect repeat sales calls after the free listing.
- Advice: take the free listing only. Add categories "Plant on rent", "Nurseries", "Landscape gardeners", "Gift shops". Track calls by number before any paid test.

### 3. Bing Places
- Free. Go to https://www.bing.com/forbusiness and use "Import from Google Business Profile". Source: https://blogs.bing.com/search/October-2025/Introducing-the-New-Bing-Places-for-Business-Built-for-Business-Owners,-Powered-by-Research
- Low traffic in India. It takes 10 minutes after item 1.

### 4. Apple Business Connect
- Free with an Apple ID. Go to https://www.apple.com/in/business/connect, claim the place, verify.
- Not verified: full India support for service-area businesses.

### 5. Houzz India
- Houzz pages say "List your business here for free": https://www.houzz.ie/professionals/landscape-contractors-and-gardening-services/c/Jaipur--IN
- Steps: create a Pro account, add business info, project photos, service area. Use real project photos only.
- Best for terrace and balcony design leads. Paid ad cost not verified.

### 6. IndiaMART
- Free registration: profile, product listing, reply to buyer enquiries. No sales commission. Source: https://help.indiamart.com/?p=35456
- Paid services (Maximiser, TrustSEAL, IM Star and others) have no public price. Source: https://help.indiamart.com/knowledge-base/paid-services-of-indiamart
- Steps: register with phone and OTP, add GST if you have it, list "Office plant rental" and "Corporate gift plants" as products with Noida price ranges.
- Risk: buyer enquiries can be resellers or sellers who look like buyers. Not verified for plant rental. Sales calls push paid plans.
- Competitor proof: other plant rental firms list on IndiaMART (example https://m.indiamart.com/growheaven/new-items.html).

### 7. Sulekha
- Free sign-up. You buy lead credits and pay per matched request. Price depends on service and location and shows only in the Sulekha Business app. Source: https://www.sulekha.com/collateral/advertise
- Steps: choose service type, enter mobile and city, claim listing, install app, set locality filter and a daily spend limit.
- Risk: you pay for a lead even if the buyer does not answer. Refund rules not verified. Start with a small credit and pause if cost per real call is high.

### 8. Social profiles
- Free. Use the same NAP. Link the site in each bio. Share office plant rental case photos. These links are mostly nofollow. Not verified per site.

### 9. TradeIndia
- Free members list company details and up to 10 pictures. Paid members get buyer contact tools. Price is not public. Source: https://www.newswire.com/news/grow-your-business-online-with-tradeindia-29011 (secondary source).
- Lower value than IndiaMART for this niche. Do it after the first 8.

### 10-13. Gardening community and blogs
- Abana Homes accepts guest posts of 1200+ words with one dofollow reference link, and answers fast. Source: https://abanahomes.com/pages/media
- Vegega: send an outline and a 150-word summary to gardener@vegega.com with subject "Submit Gardening Guest Post". Source: https://vegega.com/pages/write-for-us
- Delhi Greens: email editor@delhigreens.org. Source: https://delhigreens.com/tag/plant/ (search result).
- Write about Delhi NCR topics (hard water, low-light office plants, summer heat). Do not write a sales page. Link to a tool page on meribagiya.com.
- Noida events: the Noida Flower Festival ran 19-22 February 2026 at Shivalik Park, Sector 33A. Source: https://thebetterindia.com/culture/spring-flower-festivals-india-2026-11091166. A search result also said FSN ran a chrysanthemum show at DLF Mall of India in 2022. Stall rules and fees are not verified. Ask FSN.

### 14. Urban Company
- It lists gardening as a category. It picks partners itself and trains them. We found no open sign-up form. Not verified.
- Plant rental probably does not fit. Maintenance may fit. Look for a partner sign-up page on https://www.urbancompany.com.

### 16. Society and corporate channels
- MyGate and NoBrokerHood each claim about 25,000 societies. Sources: https://mygate.com/nobrokerhood-vs-mygate/society-management/ and https://www.nobrokerhood.com/solutions/rwa-management-software. We found no public step for a small nursery to join. Not verified.
- Better first step: contact RWA presidents and facility managers directly. Offer a free balcony garden check. Ask to put a flyer on the notice board and in the society WhatsApp group. This costs nothing. Ask for approval first, because group admins ban spam.
- For corporate: target facility managers in Noida Sector 62, 125, 135, 132 and Greater Noida offices. LinkedIn search plus a short WhatsApp message works. Not verified as a measured channel.
